body_en: |-
  If you're targeting Saudi women 18–34 and putting more budget into Meta than Snapchat, you're likely burning money. Here's the data.

  For this segment specifically:
  • CPM: Snapchat SAR 8 vs Meta SAR 17 — more than 2× cheaper reach
  • ROAS: Snapchat 4.1× vs Meta (broad) 2.1×
  • Conversion rate: 38% higher on Snapchat for this audience

  Saudi Arabia has one of the highest Snapchat penetration rates in the world, and it skews heavily toward exactly this demographic. Yet media plans are still built on global defaults that treat Meta as the default performance channel everywhere.

  My recommended allocation for GCC brands targeting this segment:
  • Google Search: 45–55% (capture existing intent)
  • Snapchat: 20–25% (highest ROAS for this audience)
  • Meta: 15–20% (retargeting only — not cold prospecting)
  • TikTok: 5–10% (testing budget, growing fast)

  The mistake isn't using Meta. It's using Meta as the default without testing whether it's actually the best-performing channel for your specific audience in this specific market.

  Test before you scale. The GCC is not a smaller version of the US or European market — the channel mix that works there doesn't transfer automatically.
